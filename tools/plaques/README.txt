Logos for the shelf / section plaques: tools/plaques/<section>.png, the section's name in lower case
with anything that isn't a letter or digit turned into "-" (e.g. "Marvel's The Avengers" ->
marvel-s-the-avengers.png). Transparent background, any size: the build fits each one onto the
standard plaque (white plate, or a dark one for a light logo). Sections without a logo get their
name on the plate. Sections are in tools/char_groups.tsv and tools/disc_groups.tsv.

The power disc sections' logos (Boosts ... Skies) are drawn by tools/section_logos.py (run it again after editing).
